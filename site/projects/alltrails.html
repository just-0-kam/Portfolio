AllTrails — Group Hike Feature

Overview

01

AllTrails is an outdoor enthusiast's companion, empowering users to explore hiking, running, and biking trails with essential information like weather updates, navigation guidance, and user reviews. This project focused on the seamless integration of a new Group Hike feature to further enhance the outdoor experience and foster community among its 6.5 million registered users.

Problem Statement

02

AllTrails users expressed a growing interest in connecting with like-minded outdoor enthusiasts to enjoy group hiking experiences. The platform lacked a way to organise, join, and coordinate group activities, leaving a gap between the app's individual trail discovery tools and the community-driven spirit of outdoor adventure.

Goals

03
01

Social Interactions

Enable hikers to connect with like-minded people by joining or creating events based on location, date, time, and group size, building a sense of community and shared experience.

02

Safety

Group hiking boosts confidence and ensures safety guidance is shared among participants, minimising risks, especially on unfamiliar or remote trails.

03

Personalised Experiences

Hikers come from different skill and fitness levels. The feature lets users join events that are the right fit for them, creating a more inclusive and tailored experience.

Research & Analysis

05

I conducted primary research on the existing AllTrails app to understand interaction patterns, interface design, and target audience needs. This was supplemented with a competitor analysis of the two closest rivals in the space.

Gaia GPS Trail finding, route recording, weather forecast. No group hike option, representing a gap in the market.
Hiking Project Crowd-sourced, North America focused. Features "Gems" (points of interest) and trail popularity sorting. Free, no group feature.

User Persona

06

Research pointed to a clear primary user: a young professional, new to a city, who loves outdoor activities but struggles to find adventure companions.

VR

Valentina Beatrice Reynolds

Age 28  ·  Software Engineer  ·  Vancouver, BC

Goals & Needs

  • Meet like-minded individuals who share her passion for hiking
  • Find and join group hikes with minimal planning friction
  • Feel safe on unfamiliar and remote trails

Pain Points

  • Limited social circle after recently moving to Vancouver
  • Demanding job leaves little time to organise outings
  • Safety concerns as a solo hiker in unfamiliar areas

Visual Design

07

I developed a style guide aligned with AllTrails' existing brand identity while giving the new feature its own visual language. The guide defined primary and secondary colour palettes and a typography system using Manrope, chosen for its modern, legible character across heading and body sizes.

Reflection

09

This project was an exciting opportunity to learn ProtoPie as a new prototyping tool. Although it took time to get up to speed, the end result was rewarding and reinforced the importance of grounding design decisions in user research. The Group Hike feature emerged directly from identified user needs, including social connection, safety, and personalised trail experiences.
