Lights & Camera

Overview

01

Lights & Camera Magazine is a Vancouver-based digital publication created to close the gap between the film industry and emerging talent. As Marketing Lead, I was responsible for growing the magazine's audience across social channels, shaping the content distribution strategy, and driving qualified traffic to the website. Editorial coverage spans film reviews, industry features, curated interviews, and a dedicated job board serving Vancouver's film community.

The Problem

02

Aspiring filmmakers in Vancouver lacked a centralized resource for industry news and job opportunities specific to the local scene. General job platforms such as Indeed returned an overwhelming volume of unrelated listings, turning the search into a stressful and inefficient exercise. L&C Magazine was designed to resolve that gap by offering a focused, accessible platform built around Vancouver's film community.

Mission & Purpose

03
Mission

Provide a platform which promotes the film industry & student work in Vancouver.

Purpose

Bridge the gap between filmmakers and film companies to grow the industry.

Services

Job Search / Curated Content / Student Showcase

Marketing Channels

04

The distribution strategy spanned four channels, each serving a distinct purpose in building the magazine's audience and reach.

01

Website

All content offered free with advertising support; the primary conversion point for newsletter sign-ups and job seekers.

02

Instagram

@landc_magazine served as the primary social channel for brand awareness and community engagement, growing to 18.5K followers.

03

YouTube

Lights & Camera MAG operated as the secondary channel, dedicated to long-form video content and on-camera interviews.

04

Newsletter

Weekly email newsletter delivering job listings, new articles, and exclusive deals to subscribers.

Social Media Growth

05

Instagram was established as the primary growth channel, with a content strategy oriented toward increasing brand awareness, driving web traffic, and cultivating a community around Vancouver's film industry. Partnerships with local film schools and production companies substantially amplified organic reach.

18.5K Instagram Followers
1,966 Posts Published
102K+ YouTube Video Likes

Brand Identity

06

The L&C visual identity was built around a high-contrast editorial palette anchored by Golden Dream yellow, a colour chosen to signal energy, creativity, and accessibility against the dark, cinematic backgrounds.

#F0CC00
#FFFFFF
#1A2820
#2B2B2B

Typography paired Telegraf Ultra Bold for headings, selected for its commanding, contemporary presence, with Writer Book for body copy, lending the editorial content a clean, readable quality free of industry jargon.

Revenue & Business Model

07

The magazine operated on a free-access model monetized through advertising and in-kind partnerships. Projected average revenue of $101,180 per year, set against operating expenses of $79,957 per year, yielded an anticipated annual profit of roughly $21,223.

$101K Avg Projected Revenue / Year
$80K Avg Projected Expenses / Year
$21K Avg Projected Profit / Year

Revenue sources comprised YouTube advertising, Instagram sponsored content, and affiliate marketing partnerships such as Canada Film Equipment, supplemented by in-kind funding from the Canada Periodical Fund, Vancouver production companies, and local film schools.

Screen Design

09

The website was designed to mirror the editorial identity: dark, cinematic backgrounds, yellow accents, and a clear content hierarchy carried consistently across the homepage, article pages, and job listings.

Home Page: The primary landing experience, featuring a dark cinematic hero, curated articles, and navigation anchored by the L&C masthead.

Article Page: The editorial reading view, combining a full-width hero image, a typographic body layout, and related content suggestions.

Jobs Page: Industry job listings integrated into the editorial experience, with filters for role type and location.

Home Page, Ad Placements: The homepage layout annotated with defined ad zones, demonstrating how sponsored content integrates without disrupting the editorial experience.
