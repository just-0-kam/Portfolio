Overview

Monocoque is an independent Formula 1 magazine developed entirely from scratch — from audience research and editorial direction through to full art direction and final print-ready layouts. The project encompassed every stage of magazine creation: defining the readership, establishing the brand, setting an editorial mandate, and producing the final designs including a cover, table of contents, feature spread, subscription page, and web ads.

The Brand

Monocoque is a French word meaning "Single Shell" — referring to the construction method used in Formula 1 cars where the body and chassis form a single structural unit. The name signals the magazine's core focus: the technical depth, engineering precision, and strategic intelligence behind the sport. Beyond the technical, Monocoque also covers team and driver performance, off-track activities, sustainability, the business and financial side of F1, and historic Grand Prix moments.

Editorial Mandate

Monocoque's mission is to give readers a comprehensive, authoritative view of Formula 1. The magazine delivers expert technical analysis, exclusive photography and illustration, behind-the-scenes access, and interviews with key figures. Coverage spans current performance, iconic historical moments, sustainability, and the future direction of the sport — always with a perspective that respects the intelligence and passion of its readership.

Audience Research

Defining the readership was the first step. The target audience are affluent, well-educated Formula 1 enthusiasts aged 25 and above — a global demographic with an active, high-income lifestyle and a genuine appetite for depth and quality.

Visual Design

The style guide was built around a bold, high-contrast palette that conveys prestige and speed. Near-black and white form the primary pair, with a vibrant yellow and a punchy red as accent colours — reflecting the drama and energy of Formula 1.

Typography pairs Cormorant Garamond for body text — lending an editorial, refined quality — with Avant Garde for headings, providing a geometric, modern contrast that feels at home in the world of motorsport.
