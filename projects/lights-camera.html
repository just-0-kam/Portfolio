Overview

Lights & Camera Magazine is a Vancouver-based digital magazine built to close the gap between the film industry and emerging talent. As the Marketing lead, I was responsible for growing the magazine's audience across social channels, developing the content distribution strategy, and driving traffic to the website. The magazine covers film reviews, industry articles, curated interviews, and a dedicated job board for Vancouver's film community.

The Problem

Aspiring filmmakers in Vancouver had no centralized resource for industry news and job opportunities specific to the local film scene. Searching on general job platforms like Indeed meant being overwhelmed by unrelated positions, making the process stressful and inefficient. L&C Magazine was built to solve exactly that — a focused, accessible platform for Vancouver's film community.

Marketing Channels

The distribution strategy spanned four channels, each serving a distinct purpose in building the magazine's audience and reach.

Website
All content free with ads — primary conversion point for newsletter sign-ups and job seekers.
Instagram
@landc_magazine — primary social channel for brand awareness and community engagement. Grew to 18.5K followers.
YouTube
Lights & Camera MAG — secondary channel for long-form video content and interviews.
Newsletter
Weekly email newsletter delivering job listings, new articles, and exclusive deals to subscribers.

Social Media Growth

Instagram was established as the primary growth channel. Content strategy focused on increasing brand awareness, driving web traffic, and building a community around Vancouver's film industry. Partnering with local film schools and production companies amplified reach.

Brand Identity

The L&C visual identity was built around a high-contrast, editorial palette anchored by Golden Dream yellow — a colour that signals energy, creativity, and accessibility against the dark, cinematic backgrounds.

Typography used Telegraf Ultra Bold for headings — commanding and modern — paired with Writer Book for body copy, giving the editorial content a clean, readable quality without industry jargon.

Revenue & Business Model

The magazine operated on a free-access model monetized through advertising and inkind partnerships. Projected average revenue of $101,180 per year against operating expenses of $79,957 per year, yielding a projected profit of approximately $21,223 per year.
