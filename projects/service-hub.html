Service Hub — Brand Identity

Overview

01

Service Hub required a complete brand identity — covering everything from logo to app design to a digital style guide. The brief called for something modern, accessible, and unmistakably professional, built to serve a diverse user base looking for home services on demand.

Design Process

03

I started with a brand strategy workshop to define personality, audience, and tone of voice. After 40+ logo sketches and 3 concept directions, we landed on a logotype that combines geometric precision with organic curves. I then built out a full visual system including colour palette, typography, iconography, and app mockups.
